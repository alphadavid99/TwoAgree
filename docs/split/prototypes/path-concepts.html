ELamps at Night

The path is the one dark screen in the app: deep claret, with a gold lamp lit for every step you finish together.

Built around
Psalm 119:105, and your brand's gold on claret
Best at
The most striking; feels set apart and reverent; lighting a lamp is a natural celebration
Weak at
Breaks the "app screens are white" rule, so it must be a deliberate exception
Seen in
Life Reset, Moonly

These are sketches of the idea, drawn in code with your colours and fonts, so the artwork is simpler than a finished screen would be. Ideas can be combined: for example the lamps from E on the road in A, or the two threads from C running through the chapters in B.
