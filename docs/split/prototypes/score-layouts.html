3The overlap

Each of you is a circle. They slide together as the number counts, and the overlap is the score.

Best at
Shows what the number means; it's about the two of you, which no quiz app has
Weak at
A low score shows two circles barely touching, which is honest but stark
Seen in
Closest is the match score in Ahead; the circles are our own idea

The top half is all celebration with the number floating in it. The details rise from the bottom on a solid white sheet.

Best at

Weak at
The celebration only has half the screen once the sheet is up

5A card to keep

The result lands as one physical-looking card in the middle of the celebration. It can be saved or shared as it is.

Best at
Everything on one solid surface; gives couples something to send each other, which helps the app spread
Weak at
The score is smaller, and sharing an agreement score may feel too private for some
Seen in
Calm, Beli, Marriott Bonvoy
